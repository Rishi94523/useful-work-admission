# Completed validation experiments

Started September 10; analyzed September 11, 2026. See
`docs/research/VINA_VALIDATION_DECISION_2026-09-11.md` for the decision and limits.

- `inputs.json`: official1iep and retained DUD-E prepared-input manifests/hashes.
- `stock_controls.json`: twelve newly measured stock E32 controls, including raw
  poses, stdout, parameters and durations. Four targets, three seeds each.
- `medium_e32.json`: new HS90A/TRYB1 matched-evaluation and nearest-time-prefix
  redocking. The earlier FA10 comparison remains in the prior evidence directory.
- `tutorial_decomposition.json`: new1iep comparison on the official20A box.
- `matched_ranking.json`: sixteen new medium-run results matched to the earlier
  normal E8 evaluation counts on the fixed4+4 subsets of FA10 and TRYB1.
- `stock_ranking_reanalysis.json`: historical full8+8 stock panels, rehashed inputs.
- `historical_stock_ranking.jsonl.gz`: original historical outputs including the
  HS90A timeout. `summary.json` records the uncompressed SHA256.
- `provisional_poisoning.json`: real original-finalizer contamination tests and
  complete molecular replays of substituted outputs.
- `deferred_policy.json`: analytical and simulated policy exposure; not deployed
  reputation measurements or fresh-computation proofs.
- `summary.json`: stock-control ranges and paired pilot ranking uncertainty.
- `environment.json`: non-sensitive local hardware/runtime context and script
  hashes. Performance measurements are not isolated speedup estimates.
- `validation_summary.png/.svg`: measured resource/redocking panels and a clearly
  labeled analytical policy panel. Rendered and visually inspected.

Expensive experiments preserve completed output. The reference build, inputs and
earlier normal-run budgets are dependencies documented in the previous checkpoint.
This directory does not establish prospective screening validation, production
admission security, or ordinary-phone performance.

# Adaptive docking follow-up: frozen evaluation plan

Recorded before the new scientific results. The outer controller is a conventional experimental component, not a bot classifier or a novelty claim. The molecular relation remains seeded Vina with full-output/trace commitments and unpredictable complete-run audits. Risk cannot excuse an inexpensive correct-output shortcut.

## Outer policy v1

Start risk R=0. Between events subtract elapsed seconds/120, floored at zero. Success subtracts 2 and decreases the consecutive failure/abandonment streak by one. Audit failure adds 18 plus min(6,2 × previous failures of an assigned range). Uncommitted abandonment adds 8; committed abandonment adds 12. Each allowed request adds 6 when more than three requests occurred in ten seconds, plus min(8,2 × current failure streak). Clamp R to [0,100]. R<15 is low, 15≤R<35 medium, 35≤R<60 high; R≥60 imposes a 60-second cooldown, reassessed after expiry. No claim is made that these thresholds are statistically optimal.

Tiers use measured Chrome configurations: low=1 ligand ×16 runs ×16,000 cap (1.348 s warm); medium=4×16×16,000 (7.448 s); high=16×16×16,000 (24.563 s). Audits sample respectively 4/8/8 complete runs. They deliberately retain increasing molecular budgets. These are historical medians, not device guarantees; cold and per-ligand preparation remain separate. One outstanding lease per identity, 16 globally, issuance token bucket capacity32/refill2 per second, and three post-commit challenges per scientific range bound outstanding work/retries. Trusted identity continuity is an assumption; identity churn and pre-application network attacks are not solved.

Evaluate normal visitors, valid high-rate traffic, 10/25/50/75/90% work, partial-cache retries, abandonment before/after commitment, and identity churn. Use actual SQLite policy transitions and separately label cost-model simulations versus actual WASM runs. Treat audits and finish verdicts as trusted server code. Count unverified scientific outputs as provisional, not as confirmed useful work.

## Scientific gates and new experiment

Outer-policy calibration amendment v2 (after v1 live-loop observation, before new science): varied ligands took 2.1–4.2 s molecular time at the initial low-tier cap16,000. Change low tier to the separately measured 1×16×4,000 browser configuration (0.332 s on the original small ligand). Keep medium/high and all risk weights unchanged. Register distinct low-cap seed ranges, never the same higher-cap prefixes. Retain v1 measurements and rerun the live loop on new ranges. The small-ligand median remains a calibration anchor, not a universal deadline guarantee.

Keep target FA10 as a development target; add DUD-E HIVPR and HSP90A as held-out pilot targets, subject to successful documented preparation. Freeze ligand selection by input order/size before docking. Use 8 actives +8 presumed decoys per added target, and independently embedded conformers generated by ETKDGv3 with fixed seeds, no crystal coordinate initialization. Retain known-bound-conformer redocking separately. Preparation failures remain in the denominator and are reported.

Compare equal nominal 256,000-evaluation search bundles: 16×16,000, 4×64,000, 1×256,000. Also test longer crystal-only independent runs up to cap1,000,000, and bounded local refinement with all starting-pose generation costs charged. Actual runtime/evaluation overshoot determines whether nominally matched configurations are in fact cost-comparable. Do not select a winner by a single target's minimum RMSD.

Exploratory pass gates: score-selected redocking RMSD≤2 Å on at least two of three targets; ranking AUC≥0.65 and no more than0.05 below same-target stock Vina E8 on at least two targets; report bootstrap intervals, top-quartile enrichment and preparation failures. These small pilots cannot establish statistical equivalence. Independent-conformer results must be disclosed even if worse. Economics gate: warm molecular fraction≥90%, full server audit cost≤20% of client total at bulk tiers, and report whether the low tier actually remains≤1.5 s once mandatory preparation is included. Failure of a gate is a result, not a reason to silently change it.

## Lightweight verification investigation

HIVPR preparation failed on modified GLZ/ASQ residues; preserve that failure and do not silently delete or rename those residues. Add **TRYB1** as a replacement held-out pilot before running any new docking. Report three prepared-target gates together with the unsuccessful HIVPR preparation, rather than claiming four successful target evaluations. All ranking inputs use the independent conformer; crystal redocking compares both source and independent conformers. The independent-conformer ranking baseline is stock E8 on the same inputs.

Search primary literature on spot-checking/credibility, verifiable computation, sumcheck/GKR and Vina before novelty claims. Test fingerprints/trace compression and counterexamples to public accumulators, local block skipping, prefix and cross-seed reuse. Hashing a fabricated transcript does not authenticate its evaluations; post-commit randomness must bind to work the server can independently check. Retain the one-use historical scientific work-credit interpretation, and measure retries under the outer controller instead of assuming unlimited attempts.

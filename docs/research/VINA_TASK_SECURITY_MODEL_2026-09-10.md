# Admission and scientific aggregation for distributed Vina tasks

The molecular result and the admission guarantee must be evaluated separately. Preserving all raw minima from each stock-shaped Monte Carlo task and applying the original global merge/refinement can preserve a monolithic result within one pinned numerical implementation. This does not make an unaudited client's result trustworthy.

## What a replay audit establishes

Let N assigned runs have equal calibrated cost, C committed outputs be correct under the pinned replay relation, and q distinct indices be sampled uniformly after commitment. Assuming incorrect outputs fail replay, the probability of acceptance is `choose(C,q)/choose(N,q)`. This is a statement about correctness of the committed output vector. It becomes a statement about attacker work only under an additional assumption that correctly reproducing a fresh task's complete output/trace requires comparable molecular computation. No benchmark proves that computational lower bound. Recording natural search traces makes a final-pose-only substitute insufficient under exact replay; it is not a cryptographic proof of time spent.

For a single assigned run, q=1 repeats its whole computation. Auditing with probability p instead gives a completely fabricated single-run contributor acceptance probability 1-p (assuming admission is granted to unaudited contributions). Auditing one session does not establish that another session computed anything. In particular, a pool of 32 runs from 32 identities with q=4 audits is not equivalent to each identity supplying 32 runs with q=4 audits. Scientific coverage can span sessions; an admission bound must identify the account/request/work-credit whose reward is at stake.

For larger bundles, replay cost can remain approximately q units while client work grows with N, but fixed q only bounds a fixed *fraction* of incorrect runs. It does not enforce every last run. At N=32 and q=4, an attacker with 28 correct outputs passes with `choose(28,4)/choose(32,4)`, approximately 0.57. Strong assurance against small omissions needs more audits. Cost-heterogeneous ligand pools also need cost-aware sampling or homogeneous cost buckets; unit fraction is not automatically compute fraction.

The existing outer admission controls remain necessary to bound challenge issuance, submission size, pending leases and replay capacity. Whole-run auditing alone is not a denial-of-service solution. A failed replay consumes molecular server work. These local experiments do not establish production concurrency or a risk classifier's effectiveness.

Fixed q can keep *molecular replay* approximately flat as N increases within a homogeneous cost bucket. It does not make the complete verifier constant-time: this commitment format carries N leaf hashes, and durable ingestion reads and stores all submitted scientific outputs. Those hashing, parsing, bandwidth and storage terms grow with the bundle. A Merkle-root opening format could reduce challenge-path communication, but would not eliminate the separate cost of ingesting useful scientific results. No such optimization is assumed in the measured implementation.

## One-use scientific credits and aggregation

The pool ledger stores each raw result and trace atomically with completion state. Completed task identities are not reissued for new credit. Canonical input hashes, engine version, receptor/grid relation, child seed, search parameters and retained-minima behavior belong in the identity. A human-facing ligand or campaign alias must not create new scientific credit. The prototype rejects registration of the same seed at another evaluation cap rather than crediting nested work independently.

Known future seeds permit precomputation. Previously performed, still-uncompleted useful work can be consumed once under a work-credit model; that is different from requiring computation after the access request. Secret unpredictable seeds reduce advance targeting, but do not prevent a party computing for others after assignment. Receptor/grid preparation is intentionally reusable and must be amortized separately. Colluders may share preparation and transfer answers; single-use global identity prevents repeated credit for the *same registered unit*, not efficient pooling of resources or shortcuts across different units. Input hashes alone do not detect chemically equivalent files re-encoded under new hashes, so campaign registration stays trusted.

Provisional aggregate readiness means all units were received. Replay-verified coverage counts the units actually replayed, and a strict consumer can require all verified outputs. A sampled bundle may contain malicious unaudited minima that distort the final ranking; top-k final-pose validation checks geometry/score but cannot recover omitted good minima or establish the whole trajectory. Later replication of selected tasks, reputation/quarantine and independent aggregates can reduce this risk. Their costs must be charged to scientific validation, not hidden from the admission verifier's economics. Universal full replay restores the reference aggregate but removes the total-compute savings.

## Difficulty and deployment interpretation

`ligands × independent runs per ligand × per-run budget` is a useful assignment description. Actual cost is a calibrated sum over ligand complexity and per-run behavior, plus input preparation, serialization, finalization and delivery. Vina's evaluation cap is not an exact duration limit and normal task length depends on ligand size/flexibility. Native timings cannot establish browser or low-end-mobile latency.

Low-risk sessions may provide one useful run under a deliberately weak/probabilistic contribution policy, while high-risk access can require a larger bundle with an explicit audit bound and outer gate. Calling both policies the same strong work-enforcement guarantee would be misleading. If even the smallest scientifically useful complete run exceeds a visitor's budget, another possible decomposition is resumable segments of a stock task. That requires serializing the full optimizer/RNG/search state and auditing dependencies; it is a different protocol with abandonment and inherited-corruption risks, not something established by this independent-run experiment.
